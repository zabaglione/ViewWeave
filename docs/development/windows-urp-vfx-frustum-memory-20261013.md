# Windows URP quilt history-budget spike while moving VFX view (2026-10-13)

## Status

This report documents a user-reported URP memory-budget event and later bounded checks in the original project. Three narrowly scoped prefab remedies are now applied: graph-reference overrides for SampleTexture2D and Sample Skinned Mesh, and `allowInstancing=false` for the CollisionAdvanced component. Their source graphs, shared Cell_01 base, scene file, and protected unrelated assets remain unchanged. On 2026-10-14 JST, the original project completed two separate native-output observations with its Balanced / Quarter profile, 12 TAA history cameras, and unchanged 4096 MB SDK budget: a 30-second SampleTexture2D center/sweep observation and a 30-second Coin-neighbor sweep. Both passed their bounded visibility, cleanup, source-protection, and native exit gates without an SDK error. The Coin raw supervisor summary still has `watcherEvidencePassed=false`; a source-independent derived contract reconciles its watcher schema fields and is audited separately without altering that raw summary. These two bounded observations do not reproduce the reported stop, determine a physical-VRAM peak or per-process VRAM attribution, or identify its cause. Longer sessions, arbitrary camera framing, other optical features, and the shutdown-crash cause remain unverified. The separate HDR intermediate implementation and its bounded passing cases remain limited to the conditions in the [HDR fix report](windows-urp-vfx-hdr-fix-20261013.md).
